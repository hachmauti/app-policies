Support

App: "Chachmaoti Ketantanim" | Hebrew learning games for children aged 3 to 7

The app does not collect data and does not send data off the device. Anything it stores stays on the device only.

Contact us

For questions, problems, or suggestions, write to us: kids.learning.games.il@gmail.com. Please mention your device type, operating system version, and what happened, so we can help quickly. Please do not send personal details about your child.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter the parents area?

The parents area is protected by a press-and-hold gesture and a math question, so young children do not enter it by accident.

How do I delete a child's profile?

In the parents area, choose the profile and press "Delete", then confirm twice. You can also delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes the stored data.

What information is stored about my child?

Only local data on the device, such as an entered nickname, a chosen character, and game progress. It is not sent anywhere and we cannot see it. We recommend using a nickname rather than a full name.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

There are no ads. An optional purchase or subscription may be offered in the future, through the app store only, and the app does not collect payment details.

I saved a backup file or teacher report. Does the app delete it?

No. A file saved on your device stays with you and the app does not delete it.

Is there an account? How do I delete data?

There is no account and no information held by us to delete. Data on the device is deleted in the parents area or by uninstalling the App.
