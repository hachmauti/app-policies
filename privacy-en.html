Privacy Policy

App: "Chachmaoti Ketantanim" (חכמאותי קטנטנים) | Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: the app does not collect data, does not send data off the device, and has no ads or analytics. Anything it stores stays on the device only.

1. Who we are

"Chachmaoti Ketantanim" (the "App") is a Hebrew-language learning games app for children aged 3 to 7. Publisher: Hachmauti, operated by Sapir Sharabi. Contact: kids.learning.games.il@gmail.com.

2. What information the App collects

The App does not collect personal information and does not transmit information to our servers or to any third party. There is no account, no sign-up, and no email address or phone number is required.

3. Information stored on the device only

To work, the App stores local data on the device where it is installed, such as child profiles (a name or nickname entered by the child or parent, and a chosen character), game progress, and preferences. This data stays on the device, is not sent anywhere, and we cannot see it. Deleting a profile in the parents area or uninstalling the App removes it. We recommend using a nickname rather than a full name. If you saved a backup file or a teacher report on your device, that file stays with you and the App does not delete it. Please do not enter sensitive or identifying information in a child's nickname or name.

4. What the App does not have

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No external links in the children's area.
  • No social features, chat, or user-generated content.
  • No use of location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics components.

5. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children aged 3 to 7 and built in line with children's privacy principles, including COPPA (United States), GDPR-K (European Union), and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law. Because we do not collect information, we do not request parental consent for collection. The parents area is protected by a press-and-hold gesture and a math question so that young children do not enter it by accident.

6. Payments and subscription

An optional monthly subscription may be offered in the future, purchasable only through the app store (Apple or Google). The store handles payment and billing. The App does not collect or store payment details. The store's privacy policy applies to the payment itself. Any such change will be reflected in this policy before it takes effect.

7. Security

Because data is stored on the device only and is not transmitted, its security depends on the security of the device itself (for example, a passcode). We recommend protecting the device with a passcode.

8. Your rights and requests

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. To delete local data, delete the profile in the parents area or uninstall the App. A backup file or report saved on your device stays with you and is not deleted by the App. For questions or requests, contact the email address above. There is no account and no information held by us to delete. Data on the device is deleted in the parents area or by uninstalling the App.

9. Privacy pages online

These pages are hosted on an external site, and the hosting provider may receive standard technical information (such as an IP address) when you view them. The App itself sends no information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. A material change, such as collecting information, will take effect only after the policy is updated and in accordance with law.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: kids.learning.games.il@gmail.com.
